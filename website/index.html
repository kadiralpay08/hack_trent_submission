Play the real Browser Boxing

Browser Boxing does not allow itself to be shown inside another page, so it opens in its own window, placed right over this area. Your punches reach it through a small program on your laptop that presses the keys.

  1. Start the key bridge. In the project folder, run:
    python keybridge.py --live
    Looking for the bridge…
  2. Open the game. Choose casual play or a bot, never ranked.
  3. Click inside the game once so it has the keyboard, then start a fight.
  4. Turn on "Send keys" in the side panel, or press F8 from any window.

Keys start OFF so stray movements never type into another window. They are released when you leave the picture, stop the camera, or close this page.

Your camera shows here with a skeleton drawn on you, so you can see where you stand and stay in the picture.

The video stays on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.

Keys for Browser Boxing

The key bridge is not running.

With the bridge running, F7 calibrates, F8 turns keys on or off, and F9 stops them. These work from any window.

How to move
HookSwing a hand in toward the middle of your body
UppercutHand low, then drive it up
Jab, crossA camera in front cannot see these. Use the sparring game's J and K keys, or play with the glove in play.bat
BlockBoth hands up by your face
DuckDrop your head
SlipLean your head left or right

No camera? In the sparring game, J K H U throw punches and W S A D block, duck and slip.
